Questions

What people ask first.

How much does custom software cost in South Africa?
It depends on what the software has to do. Tell us the dream and we'll give you a clear, fair price before any work starts.
What kind of software can you build?
Web apps, business apps, lead generation systems, AI automation, staff tools, dashboards and websites. If you can describe it, ask us.
Do I need to understand technology?
No. Explain the problem the way you'd explain it to a friend, and we'll work out the technical side.
What happens after the software goes live?
We host it, fix it and keep it updated. Small changes are part of the service, and bigger features are quoted separately.
Do you only work in Gauteng?
We're based in the Vaal Triangle, and everything we build runs online, so we can work with businesses across South Africa.
Can you handle personal information properly?
Yes. Where an app holds personal information, we build in secure logins and POPIA consent from the start.
